# The games

The 18 game databases in this folder are Scott Adams's text adventures, **© Scott Adams, 1978–1985**. They are not public domain, and the MIT license that covers SABAL's code does not cover them.

Scott Adams distributes the original text-only games as **shareware**: they may be freely downloaded and enjoyed, and paying for them is voluntary. His shareware notice, as it appears in the collection these files come from, reads:

> These Scott Adams original text Adventure games are still copyrighted
> by Scott Adams and are not Public Domain. They may be freely downloaded
> and enjoyed though. They are shareware but the contribution is strictly
> voluntary and the amount left to your own discretion.
>
> If you are unable or unwilling to pay for these games you may still
> freely enjoy them.

If you enjoy them, consider supporting Scott Adams. His site is [msadams.com](https://www.msadams.com/), and its [downloads page](https://www.msadams.com/downloads.htm) has playable versions, hint sheets and more. The payment address in the 1997 notice may be out of date, so check the site first.

## Where these files come from

They are unchanged copies of **The Scott Adams Collection 2.2** (`AdamsGames.zip`, 1997) from the [Interactive Fiction Archive](https://ifarchive.org/indexes/if-archive/scott-adams/games/scottfree). That collection's conversion to the "ScottFree" format, and its readme, are by **Paul David Doherty**. His readme is included unchanged as [`0readme.txt`](0readme.txt), with the full shareware notice, the version of each game and playing hints.
